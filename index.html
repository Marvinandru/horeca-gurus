Commercial Kitchen Operations SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures)

Operating HORECA Gurus with the speed, precision, and SLA discipline of an on-demand logistics network — engineered specifically for high-stress commercial kitchens and luxury food service.

🚀 Sub-60 Min Kitchen SLA
⚡

1. Dual Fulfillment: Dawn Haul vs "Kitchen 911" Express

Commercial kitchens have two distinct supply demands that traditional vendors fail to satisfy:

  • Scheduled Morning Bulk (5:00 AM – 7:30 AM): Consolidated van deliveries of daily staples (potatoes, onions, carcass meats, bulk tilapia) dropped before prep cooks arrive.
  • "Kitchen 911" On-Demand Rush (30–60 Min SLA): When a restaurant gets an unexpected Friday evening party and runs out of Tiger Prawns, Ribeye, or Hass Avocados, our rapid motorcycle/caddy runners dispatch emergency replenishment in under an hour.
📦

2. Color-Coded Euro-Crates & Zero-Break Cold Chain

Zero dirty sacks, zero cardboard disintegrating in walk-in chillers. Goods arrive in food-grade, sanitized, tamper-evident Euro-crates:

  • 🟢 Green Crates: Grade 1 farm produce (Nyandarua potatoes, Mwea tomatoes, Kinangop snow peas) sorted and aerated.
  • 🔴 Red Crates: Abattoir-inspected Halal beef, lamb, and goat cuts vacuum-packed or wrapped.
  • 🔵 Blue Insulated Tubs: Fresh coastal seafood and Lake Victoria catch submerged on drained wet-flake ice slurries (0°C to 2°C).
⛔

3. Strict Zero-Credit Policy: 100% Cashflow Protection

Traditional Kenyan food suppliers go bankrupt waiting for 60–90 day hotel invoices. HORECA Gurus enforces strict instant liquidity:

  • 100% Advance PO Deposit: Recurring accounts deposit funds into their PO ledger (e.g. KES 500,000); every morning drop automatically draws down the balance with instant WhatsApp receipts.
  • Payment on Delivery (POD): First-time orders settle via M-Pesa Till before the delivery runner cuts the tamper seal on the crates.
  • Zero Credit Debt: Outstanding client debt remains strictly KES 0.00 across the entire operation.
🎁

4. The "Chef's Tasting Basket" Conversion Engine

Never cold pitch a contract over the phone. Chefs buy what they can inspect on their cutting boards:

  1. The 60-Second Call (10:00–11:30 AM): Call before lunch service. Offer a free chef tasting basket (10kg Shangi potatoes, 5kg Mwea tomatoes, fresh ocean prawns/tilapia).
  2. The 6:00 AM Delivery Drop: Delivered in sanitized green/blue crates with a laminated price index and WhatsApp QR code.
  3. Autonomous 1-Tap Ordering: Chef tests during morning prep, loves the zero-waste quality, taps WhatsApp link, and funds their initial PO deposit.
🛡️

5. Instant Replacement & 45-Minute Quality Guarantee

Building iron-clad trust with Michelin-trained chefs requires zero friction when expectations aren't met:

  • Snap & WhatsApp: Receiving staff inspects goods at bay. If any tomato is bruised or fish doesn't meet temp, they snap a photo to our dispatch desk.
  • 45-Minute Dispatch SLA: An emergency runner delivers Grade 1 replacement within 45 minutes across Nairobi dining hubs.
  • Instant PO Credit: If preferred, immediate credit adjustment is applied to their PO ledger before invoice close.
✈️

6. Agricultural Air-Freight Gateway (JKIA to EU/UAE)

Exporting avocados, snow peas, fine beans, and chillies via Nairobi JKIA Cargo Terminal:

  • HCD & KEPHIS Certification: Registered with AFA Horticultural Crops Directorate; phytosanitary certificates issued at Cargo Village.
  • GlobalG.A.P. Traceability: Audited outgrowers with zero banned pesticides and MRL laboratory verification.
  • Pre-Cooling: Hydro-cooling snow peas to 2°C and avocados to 5.5°C within 4 hours of picking.
