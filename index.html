Kenya HoReCa Supply & Export Operating Manual

A strategic guide on acquiring hotel accounts, converting cold restaurant leads into recurring online buyers, managing early morning logistics, and executing international export shipments.

1. The "Tasting Basket" Funnel

Top executive chefs receive dozens of calls weekly. Never sell them a contract on cold call 1. Instead, follow this 3-step conversion path:

  1. The 60-Second Call: Call between 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM (prep window). Offer a complimentary Chef's Tasting Basket (10kg Shangi potatoes, 5kg Grade 1 Mwea salad tomatoes, 2kg Lake Victoria Tilapia).
  2. The 6:00 AM Delivery Drop: Deliver the sample basket directly to their receiving bay with a printed pricing card and a QR code linking to this web portal.
  3. The Follow-up WhatsApp: Chef receives the link on WhatsApp. With 1 tap, they can order their next morning delivery or set up a recurring standing order.

2. Nairobi Cold-Chain Route Dispatch

HoReCa kitchens require goods before breakfast or lunch prep begins. Missing the 7:30 AM window causes food prep delays.

  • 8:00 PM (Previous Night): Cut-off for online portal orders. Automatic picking slips generated.
  • 3:30 AM: Goods arrive from Nyandarua and Kisumu cold trucks at Nairobi central packing depot (Industrial Area / City Market).
  • 4:30 AM: Quality grading, weighing, and sorting into sanitized food-grade crates.
  • 5:15 AM - 7:30 AM: Route A (Westlands & Parklands), Route B (Kilimani & Hurlingham), Route C (CBD & Upper Hill).

3. Agricultural Export Gateway (KEPHIS & HCD)

For shipping avocados, snow peas, fine beans, and chillies to the EU, UK, and UAE:

  • HCD Dealer License: Register with Horticultural Crops Directorate (AFA).
  • KEPHIS Phytosanitary Certificate: Every export consignment must undergo phytosanitary inspection at JKIA Cargo Village.
  • GlobalG.A.P. Farm Traceability: Ensure outgrower farmers in Murang'a and Kinangop are audited with zero banned chemical residues.
  • Pre-Cooling: Hydro-cool avocados to 5.5°C and snow peas to 2°C within 4 hours of harvest.
