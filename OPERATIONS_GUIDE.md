## 2. Converting Cold Restaurant Leads into Recurring Online Buyers

### The Dilemma
Head Chefs and Food & Beverage (F&B) Managers in Nairobi receive calls from vendors every day. They already have existing suppliers ("mama mbogas" or brokers at Marikiti). If you call asking to sell them potatoes or beef, they will almost always say: *"We are sorted, thank you."*

### The Winning 3-Stage Acquisition Strategy

```
┌─────────────────────────┐      ┌─────────────────────────┐      ┌─────────────────────────┐
│ STAGE 1: THE 60-SEC CALL│ ───► │ STAGE 2: CHEF'S BASKET  │ ───► │ STAGE 3: DIGITAL ONBOARD│
│ 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM     │      │ 6:00 AM Delivery Drop   │      │ Late-night 1-tap reorder│
│ Pitch "Backup & Price"  │      │ 10kg Shangi, Tilapia,   │      │ WhatsApp PO dispatch    │
│ Offer tasting basket    │      │ Tomatoes with QR portal │      │ 14/30-day invoice terms │
└─────────────────────────┘      └─────────────────────────┘      └─────────────────────────┘
```

#### Step 1: The 60-Second Telephone Pitch (Call between 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM)
*Why this time?* Kitchen service runs 12:30 PM - 3:30 PM. Before 10:00 AM chefs are inspecting morning deliveries. The golden window is 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM.

> **Script:**
> *"Good morning Chef [Name], this is [Your Name] from HORECA Gurus Supply. I know your kitchen is in the middle of prep, so I'll be very brief.
> We deliver graded Nyandarua potatoes, Mwea salad tomatoes, aged Boran beef, and overnight Lake Victoria catch directly to premier kitchens across [Westlands / Kilimani / CBD].
> We fix your prices on 30-day corporate contracts so you avoid Marikiti rainy season price surges, and we guarantee 5:30 AM delivery with a 100% zero-rejection policy.
> I'd like to drop off a complimentary Chef's Tasting Basket this Thursday morning at 6:00 AM—with 10kg of Nyandarua potatoes, 5kg Grade 1 Mwea salad tomatoes, and fresh Lake Tilapia fillets for your prep team to test. Which morning works best for your receiving staff?"*

#### Step 2: Overcoming the Common Objections

- **Objection: "We already have a supplier."**
  - *Response:* *"We completely understand Chef! The top kitchens we supply (like Ankole Grill and Tamarind) keep us as their primary cold-chain backup for when their regular vendor faces rainy season shortages, price hikes, or late 9:00 AM deliveries. Let us just send the sample basket so you have our direct ordering link on file when you need emergency morning replenishment."*

- **Objection: "Your prices might be higher than Marikiti."**
  - *Response:* *"When you factor in the 15-20% kitchen sorting loss from Marikiti sacks (rotten potatoes, bruised tomatoes) plus transport hassle, our pre-graded Grade 1 crates actually save you 12% in usable food cost. Plus, we absorb price spikes on monthly contracts."*

#### Step 3: Transition to Autonomous Online Ordering
Once the chef tests the sample:
1. Send them a WhatsApp link to your web portal: `https://your-domain.ke`
2. Their kitchen supervisor or sous chef can tap the link at 10:30 PM after dinner service and submit their order in 30 seconds.
3. The order dispatches to your WhatsApp desk and automatically queues into your morning packing sheet.

## 4. Agricultural Export Compliance Checklist (KEPHIS & HCD)

For exporting avocados, snow peas, fine beans, and fresh chillies to the EU, UK, and UAE:

1. **Horticultural Crops Directorate (HCD) License:**
   - Register as an authorized horticultural exporter with AFA (Agriculture and Food Authority).
2. **KEPHIS Phytosanitary Certificate:**
   - Mandatory inspection for pests (e.g., False Codling Moth *Thaumatotibia leucotreta* on chillies and capsicums, Fruit Fly on avocados).
   - Issued at the KEPHIS JKIA Cargo Terminal inspection center.
3. **GlobalG.A.P. Certification:**
   - Required by EU and UK supermarket buyers. Covers Maximum Residue Limits (MRL) for pesticides and worker hygiene.
4. **Cold Chain Protocol:**
   - Hass Avocados: Rapid pre-cooling to 5.5°C within 12 hours of harvest. Controlled atmosphere (CA) reefer container (4% O2, 6% CO2).
   - Snow Peas & Fine Beans: Hydro-cooling to 2°C immediately post-harvest. Pack in 4kg corrugated export cartons. Air freighted from JKIA within 24–36 hours.
